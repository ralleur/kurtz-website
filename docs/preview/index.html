02.2 / THE OPTIONAL PARTS

And even more

As much as you need.
As little as you like.

Start with the parts that make life easier. Add more when you want to. You decide what to connect and what your assistant can access.

Local AIAn assistant on your own hardware.+

Ask questions, work through an idea or get help with the content you choose to share. Local models run on your own hardware. External AI remains an explicit choice.

LibraryYour films and series, ready to play.+

Browse your collection, open a title and pick up where you left off. A familiar place to decide what to watch, with your own films and series at the centre.

GalleryA place to return to your favourite moments.+

Browse your photos and albums, open an original and revisit the moments you saved. Your memories, at home alongside the rest of your digital life.

Home networkYour setup, clearly documented.+

Keep the documentation for your devices, services and setup within reach. Search your notes or ask the assistant, with links back to the source.

Agent jobsAutomate the tasks you repeat.+

Schedule document checks, assistant tasks or actions in services you connect. You choose the task, timing and permitted access. Results and problems stay visible, so you can keep track.

These capabilities are part of the private development preview ↗.

Built around a simple belief

Your digital life.
Actually yours.

Your private life should have a home that belongs to you. kurtz is being built to keep your collection and your AI on hardware you control.

With local AI, your private content doesn’t have to travel to an AI provider to be useful.

What runs where? +

The first version is being developed around an Apple Silicon Mac at home, with access through kurtz on Mac, iPhone, iPad and in the browser.

Connected services, such as your email provider, still have their own data arrangements. External AI is an optional, explicit choice. kurtz doesn’t automatically switch from a local model to the cloud.
