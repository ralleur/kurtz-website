PERSONAL MEANS PERSONAL

Your life.
Your hardware.
Your say.

Help with your private life shouldn’t require handing it over.

kurtz is being built around storage and AI on hardware you control. Your assistant can work with your content there, without sending it to an AI cloud.

You choose what it can access. Sources stay within reach. Actions that need your confirmation stay yours to decide.

A little more detail +

The first version is being developed around an Apple Silicon Mac at home, with kurtz on Mac, iPhone, iPad and in the browser.

External AI services are an optional, explicit choice. There is no automatic switch from local AI to a cloud provider. Services you connect, such as your existing email account, keep their own data arrangements.

THOUGHT THROUGH. THEN SIMPLIFIED.

Everything you need.
Less in your way.

The idea is simple: keep the capability, take away as much complexity as possible.

Everyday words. Fewer things to configure and maintain. Technical details there when you want them, quietly out of the way when you don’t.

More room for the things you actually came to do.

Can I try kurtz yet?+

There is no public download for the full kurtz app yet. A release date and pricing haven’t been announced.

Is this the video player?+

kurtz is the broader home for your digital life. kurtz video player is available separately: a free, complete player for local videos and Jellyfin. It works on its own.

Where does the benchmark fit?+

gassi benchmark is a separate place to explore and compare local AI models. You won’t need to understand benchmarks to use kurtz.
